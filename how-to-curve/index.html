HOW TO CURVE THE BALL IN ILLEGAL SOCCER

The definitive tutorial on mastering curve shots, mouse drag angles, controller triggers, and lethal airborne Q-Flick volley combos.

⚡ Quick Curve Instructions

  • PC (Keyboard/Mouse): Hold Middle Mouse Button and drag cursor left or right while executing your kick (Left Click).
  • Controller (Xbox/PlayStation): Hold LT and RT together, then tilt the Right Analog Stick in the direction of the desired spin.
  • Pro Combo: Tap Q to pop the ball into the air, then immediately perform the curve shot for an unblockable dipping strike.
PC STEP-BY-STEP

PC Keyboard & Mouse Curve Shot Walkthrough

On PC, curving relies on smooth horizontal mouse drag motion while holding your curve keybind.

🖱️ PC Execution Breakdown:

  1. Position Your Avatar: Approach the ball with momentum. Ensure your crosshair is aimed roughly 2-3 yards outside the goalpost.
  2. Hold Left Click (Power): Charge your shot power bar to 60-80%.
  3. Hold Middle Mouse Button: Simultaneously press and hold the Middle Mouse Button (scroll wheel click).
  4. Drag Horizontally:
    • Drag Cursor Left: Generates inside curve toward the left side of the net.
    • Drag Cursor Right: Generates outside "banana / trivela" curve toward the right.
  5. Release to Shoot: Release Left Click to unleash the curling projectile.
CONTROLLER GUIDE

Controller (Xbox / PlayStation) Curve Inputs

Console and gamepad players use dual-trigger input combined with analog stick steering:

🎮 Gamepad Instructions:

  1. Hold down LT (Left Trigger) and RT (Right Trigger) at the same time as you approach the ball.
  2. A curve trajectory arc indicator will appear on your HUD.
  3. Use the Right Thumbstick to push left or right to set the degree of bend.
  4. Press the Shoot button (B on Xbox / Circle on PS) to strike the ball.
PRO TECHNIQUES

Advanced Curve Shot Tactics

1. The Q-Flick Volley (Unblockable)

Press Q to flick the ball upward over sliding defenders, then immediately unleash a curved volley. High airborne curves dip viciously under the top bar.

2. Aim for the Outer Post

Never aim directly at the goalkeeper. Aim 2-3 yards outside the post and let the game's curve physics bend the ball into the top corner.

3. Adjust Curve Sensitivity

If your shots are over-bending out of bounds, lower your Curve Sensitivity in Settings to 40-50% for tight, controlled swerve.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Curve Shot FAQ

Can I rebind the Curve key on PC?

Yes! In the game's Settings menu, click on Keybinds and remap Curve from Middle Mouse Button to a side mouse button or any keyboard key (e.g. C or V).

Does curve work with passes as well as shots?

Yes. You can hold the curve button while performing a ground pass (Right Click) or lob (F) to bend passes around blocking opponents.
