How to Curve the Ball in Illegal Soccer

The curve shot is the most lethal scoring technique in Illegal Soccer. By bending the trajectory around goalkeepers and defensive walls, you can score from virtually any angle on the pitch.

🎯 The 3 Golden Rules of Curving:

  1. PC Execution: Hold down the Middle Mouse Button as you charge or release your Left Click shot, then glide your mouse sideways in the intended bend direction.
  2. Airborne Flick Combo (Q + Curve): Tap Q to pop the ball chest-high, then immediately release a curved volley. High-altitude curves drop sharply under the crossbar.
  3. Aim at the Outer Post: Never aim directly at the center of the net when curving. Aim 2-3 yards wide of the goal and let physics curl it into the top corner.

Ranked Mode: Requirements & Legal vs Illegal Playlists

Once you understand the basic mechanics, competitive Ranked mode is where serious players grind for leaderboard glory.

🏆 Ranked Qualification & Mode Differences:

  • Unlock Threshold: You must own at least 5 items in your inventory (each item roll costs 1,000 Coins in the shop).
  • Legal Ranked: Pure soccer skill without any item usage. No weapons, jetpacks, or explosives allowed. Great for precision passing and dribbling purists.
  • Illegal Ranked: All unlocked weapons, gadgets, and items are active. Fast-paced, chaotic, and heavily dependent on gadget timing and team synergy.

Illegal Soccer FAQ

How do I unlock new items in Illegal Soccer?

You can purchase Item Rolls in the in-game Shop for 1,000 Coins each, or complete the beginner tutorial by scoring 3 goals for initial free rewards.

What is the best setting to change immediately?

Turn ON Assisted Passes in the settings menu. This makes your right-click passes automatically snap toward your nearest open teammate.

Can you tackle or punch players without the ball?

Yes! In Illegal Soccer there are no referees or fouls. You can punch, tackle, or use weapons on any opponent at any time.
